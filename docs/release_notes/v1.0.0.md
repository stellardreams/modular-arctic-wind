## 💡 Engineering Philosophy: Simple Innovation & Extreme Resourcefulness

> *“We haven't got the money, so we've got to think.”*  
> — **Ernest Rutherford**, Nobel Prize-winning physicist

Remote high-latitude installations and austere Arctic operational bases rely heavily on diesel fuel transported via seasonal sealift barge or winter ice-road convoys. Rather than requiring 100-ton commercial cranes or warm-weather concrete foundations, the **Modular Arctic Wind Turbine** self-contains its entire lifecycle inside a single standard **20 ft ISO container** ($9,216.8\text{ kg} \le 10,886\text{ kg}$ payload limit), deploys via an integrated roof A-frame gin-pole winch ($0^\circ \to 90^\circ$), and operates continuously down to **$-60\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$**.

## 🏗️ Consolidated Architectural Progression

### Phase I: `v1.0` Two-Phase Rocking Sail & Disproof Audit (`cad/openscad/archive/v1.0/`)
- **Issues:** [`modular-arctic-wind#1`](https://github.com/stellardreams/modular-arctic-wind/issues/1) (`asi.surge.sh#82`), [`#2`](https://github.com/stellardreams/modular-arctic-wind/issues/2) (`asi.surge.sh#83`), [`#3`](https://github.com/stellardreams/modular-arctic-wind/issues/3) (`asi.surge.sh#84`)
- Evaluated a vertical rocking sail stabilized by liquid/thermosyphon cells (`wind_sail_v5_arctic.scad`).
- **Rigorous First-Principles Disproof:**
  1. **Aerodynamic Damping Deficit (`r3_aero.py`)**: Aerodynamic damping was $0.014\% - 0.656\%$ of structural damping; the sail weathervaned rather than oscillating.
  2. **Cryogenic Freezing & Toppling (`r1_stability_sweep.py`)**: At $-60\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$, water/glycol columns froze downward, reducing restoring stiffness below $\mu_{\text{crit}}$ and causing winter toppling.
- Preserved in `cad/openscad/archive/v1.0/` as an auditable record of disproved hypotheses that motivated the pivot to a horizontal-axis containerized turbine.

---

### Phase II: `v2.0` 50 kW Containerized Horizontal-Axis Wind Turbine & 12-Model Physics Suite (`cad/openscad/v2.0/`)
- **Issues:** [`#4`](https://github.com/stellardreams/modular-arctic-wind/issues/4) (`#85`), [`#5`](https://github.com/stellardreams/modular-arctic-wind/issues/5) (`#86`), [`#6`](https://github.com/stellardreams/modular-arctic-wind/issues/6) (`#87`), [`#7`](https://github.com/stellardreams/modular-arctic-wind/issues/7) (`#88`), [`#8`](https://github.com/stellardreams/modular-arctic-wind/issues/8) (`#96`)
- **5-Package Hardware Architecture:**
  - **Package 1 (Foundation):** 20 ft ISO container base sled + $4\times$ fold-out outriggers + helical screw anchors ($\text{FoS} = 2.45 \ge 1.50$).
  - **Package 2 (Mast & Erection):** $24.0\text{ m}$ sectional steel mast ($5 \times 4.8\text{ m}$ flanged tubes, $\varnothing 508\text{ mm}$) + roof A-frame gin-pole winch ($48.2\text{ kN}$ peak cable tension, Euler buckling $\text{FoS} = 3.82$).
  - **Package 3 (Powertrain):** Direct-drive permanent magnet synchronous generator (PMSG, $331.8\text{ kg}$ active mass, $+21.5\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ self-heating above $-60\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ ambient).
  - **Package 4 (Segmented Rotor & Microgrid):** $26.0\text{ m}$ 3-bladed rotor split into modular segments ($\le 1.90\text{ m}$) with carbon-fiber box-spar sleeve joints, leading-edge electro-thermal de-icing, and a $400\text{ V}$ 3-phase AFE inverter + $50\text{ kWh}$ BESS displacing **$21,087\text{ L/year}$** of Arctic diesel.
  - **Package 5 (Verification Harness):** 12 standalone Python physics verification scripts (`./scripts/test_wind.sh` — **12/12 PASS**).

### Phase III: `v3.0` / `v3.1` FreeCAD Parametric OpenCASCADE B-Rep Solid Models & Kinematics (`cad/freecad/`)
- **Issues:** [`#9`](https://github.com/stellardreams/modular-arctic-wind/issues/9) (`#103`), [`#10`](https://github.com/stellardreams/modular-arctic-wind/issues/10) (`#104`), [`#11`](https://github.com/stellardreams/modular-arctic-wind/issues/11) (`#111`)
- Ported the entire architecture to analytical OpenCASCADE B-Rep solid geometry via FreeCAD 1.0.0 (`cad/freecad/modular_wind_turbine_v3.py`) and exported monolithic ISO 10303 STEP solids (`cad/step_exports/modular_wind_turbine_v3.step`).
- **Key `v3.1` Refinements:**
  - 28-point discretized NACA 4415/4418 lofted blades with continuous chord taper ($1.20\text{ m} \to 0.45\text{ m}$) and $10^\circ \to 1^\circ$ wash-out twist.
  - 4-point guyed mast tension ring at $Z = 16.0\text{ m}$ with clevis lug ears, turnbuckles, and $24\text{ mm}$ steel cables.
  - Helical anchor drive caps, shear-pin outrigger hinges, and crane-less gin-pole winch rigging.
  - Multi-stage deployment stills, $360^\circ$ solid turntables, and cooperative `STRIDER-M` + `CORA-M` field erection animations.
